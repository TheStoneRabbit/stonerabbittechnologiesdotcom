How I work

Diagnose first. Build only if building is the answer.

AI writes a lot of code these days — the difference is knowing what's worth writing at all. Every line that does get written is reviewed, tested, and integrated by an actual engineer.

01

Diagnose

We start with what's actually going wrong, not a tech stack. Often the real problem isn't the one you called about.

02

Right-size the fix

A setting, a tool you already pay for, an automation, or a real build — in that order. Quoted at a fixed price so scope can't drift.

03

Build & test

AI to move fast, engineering judgement to steer it. Secure, tested, and built to be maintained rather than demoed.

04

Hand over

Deployed, documented, and explained — so you aren't dependent on me. Ongoing support if you want it, not because you're stuck.

My Grandson The IT Guy

Most of what I build is for businesses. This one isn't. It's a monthly service for older and non-technical people — looking after their email, their passwords, and the accounts with their name on them, so a scam call or a forgotten login doesn't turn into something much worse.

It runs on the same engineering: automated breach monitoring, an AI-assisted scam-check inbox, and a plain-English report that goes to the client and their family every month. Delivered entirely by phone and screen share, and deliberately limited to a small number of clients.
